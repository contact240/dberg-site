Why it exists

The audit surfaces the gaps. It doesn't tell you which ones matter.

Your readiness audit shows where you're exposed. What it can't give you is prioritization, sequencing, and the real cost of each gap — the three things that decide whether your entry compounds value or compounds cost. The Entry Diagnostic is where that reading happens: a structured diagnosis, run personally, that turns a list of gaps into a critical path.

It does not give you prioritization, sequencing, or local execution. This is where the first two begin.
The 90 minutes

A structured diagnosis across five risk dimensions.

Not a sales call — a working session. I read your entry across the five dimensions of the Brazil Readiness Framework, pressure-test your thesis, and isolate the two or three gaps sitting on your critical path. You bring the operation; I bring the structure.

01

Market Intelligence

Whether your read of the Brazilian market is built for Brazil — or transposed from another. ICP, purchasing behavior, regional variance, willingness to pay.

02

Operational Backbone

The rails that carry growth: legal presence, fiscal structure, payment and banking infrastructure, the HQ ↔ Brazil operating model.

03

Cultural & Legal Adaptation

Where surface localization ends and structural exposure begins — language, relational tone, LGPD, CDC-compliant terms.

04

Conversion Engine

Whether traffic becomes revenue: mobile-first funnel, WhatsApp as infrastructure, localized checkout, local proof.

05

Strategic Ecosystem

The relationships that compress years into months: local partners, advisors, presence, and the introductions that open the market.

You leave the session knowing your critical path. You receive the sequence in writing.
